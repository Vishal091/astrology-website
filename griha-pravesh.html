Why Griha Pravesh Muhurat is Important

Griha Pravesh is one of the most sacred housewarming rituals in Hindu tradition. The ceremony symbolizes purification of the new home and the beginning of a peaceful, prosperous, and harmonious life.

According to Vedic beliefs, entering a new home during an auspicious Muhurat helps attract positive energies, spiritual blessings, and long-term stability for the family members.

Vastu Tips For Griha Pravesh

According to Vastu Shastra, the main entrance of the home should remain clean, bright, and welcoming during Griha Pravesh. Lighting diyas, performing puja, and maintaining positive energy are considered highly auspicious.
