Why Mundan Sanskar is Important

Mundan Sanskar, also known as Chudakarana, is one of the important Hindu rituals performed during early childhood. The ceremony symbolizes purification, spiritual growth, and positive beginnings for the child.

According to Vedic traditions, selecting an auspicious Mundan Muhurat based on Panchang, Nakshatra, and planetary alignments is believed to bring prosperity, protection, and healthy development.
